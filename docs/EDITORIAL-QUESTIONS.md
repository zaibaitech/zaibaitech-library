## Mukhtasar al-Akhdari

### A1. Working water between the toes (wudu, passage 6)

- **Passage:** `wudu_faraid_sunan.p06`
- **Arabic (OpenITI, also the Subayh printing):** ويجب تخليل أصابع الرجلين — "it is obligatory to work water between the toes".
- **Published English (en-zaibaitech-2026):** "It is obligatory to work water between the fingers (takhlil), and recommended to do so between the toes. …"
- **Translator's note (published with the passage):** the translation follows the relied-upon (mashhur) Maliki position, citing Khalil's *Mukhtasar*: «بتخليل أصابعه» for the hands and «وندب تخليل أصابعهما» for the feet.
- **Glossary, entry kaʿb (editorial note, unreviewed):** "The same chapter says the toes must be passed through." This follows the Arabic, so it disagrees with the English.
- **The question:** should the English render the Arabic as written (toes obligatory) and give the mashhur position in the note? Or keep the present rendering with its note? Either way, the glossary entry should be made consistent with the decision.
- **Status:** open. Raised in the preview QA review (finding #8), October 2026.

### A2. Vowelling of the same passage

- **Vowelled text (OpenITI diacritized witness):** وَيَجِبُ تَخْلِيلِ أَصْابِعُ الرَجْلِينَ
- **The question:** the case endings look wrong for the sentence (تخليلُ as subject, أصابعِ in construct), and الرِّجْلَيْنِ lacks its shadda and sukun. Should the vowelling be corrected? If so, record the correction in `CORRECTIONS.md` with its reference.
- **Status:** open. Noted while recording A1; not changed.
